[deliberate] The final step is purposely human. The agent calls commit intake booking with a therapist ID, a selected slot, and a de-identified summary. TheraSync locks the slot for ten minutes, then opens an editable approval card. At this point, no booking exists. Decline releases the lock. Approve and sign sends the exact lock token with user consent. The server validates both, writes a confirmed booking to PostgreSQL, and a uniqueness rule prevents a second confirmed booking for that therapist and slot. The confirmation banner appears only after the commit succeeds. Notifications and calendar entries shown in the demo are illustrative; the safety and approval gates are not.
