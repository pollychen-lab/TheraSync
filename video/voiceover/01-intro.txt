[warmly] Welcome to TheraSync. Traditional mental-health intake can force someone through rigid forms before anyone checks for crisis language, or for a slot that can recur. TheraSync uses WebMCP to expose two typed browser tools: triage and booking. In DevTools, the registry is readable before an agent acts. The browser gives an agent structured access; the server owns safety checks, slot locks, consent, and durable records. The principle is simple: agents move the workflow forward, while people keep the decision.
